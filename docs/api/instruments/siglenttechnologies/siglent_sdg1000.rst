####################################
Siglent SDG1000 Waveform Generator
####################################

Two-channel function/arbitrary waveform generator with 14-bit DAC,
125 MSa/s sample rate, and 16 kpts of arbitrary waveform memory. Sine
bandwidth depends on the SKU and ranges from 5 MHz (SDG1005) to 50 MHz
(SDG1050).

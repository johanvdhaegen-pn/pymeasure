####################################
Siglent SDG1000X Waveform Generator
####################################

Two-channel function/arbitrary waveform generator with 14-bit DAC,
150 MSa/s sample rate, and 16 kpts of arbitrary waveform memory. Available
SKUs are SDG1032X (30 MHz sine) and SDG1062X (60 MHz sine).
